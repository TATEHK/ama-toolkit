What is Black Belt Club?

Black Belt Club is our elite program for students serious about earning their black belt — advanced training beyond the regular classes, real skills, and a community for all ages held to a higher standard of honour, respect and confidence.

  • Advanced training beyond the regular classesWeapons (bo staff and nunchucks), knife defence, Wavemaster power work and higher-level techniques reserved for Black Belt Club members — not taught in regular classes.
  • Train like a black belt every weekExtra classes built on the black belt syllabus — sharper, more accurate technique and faster progress.
  • Push past your limitsBalance, acrobatics and athletic conditioning that take your training up a level.
  • A community for all agesFrom kids to Black Belt Mums, training together on a clear path to black belt.
