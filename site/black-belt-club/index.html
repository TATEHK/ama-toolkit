What is Black Belt Club?

Black Belt Club is our commitment program for students serious about earning their black belt. It's extra training, extra attention, and a community that holds you to a higher standard.

  • Exclusive Black Belt Club classesExtra weekly training built around the black belt syllabus — faster progress, sharper technique.
  • Priority at gradings & eventsFirst access to gradings, seminars and special training days with our senior instructors.
  • Leadership & mentoringGrow as a leader on the mats and help guide the students coming up behind you.
  • A clear path to black beltA structured journey with the people and the plan to get you there.
