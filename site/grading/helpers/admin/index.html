Black belt grading day sign-ups

Turn someone on to make their personal sign-up link, then tap Send to text and email it to them (you'll see the messages first). Nothing goes until you tap Send. Little Dragons gradings only show to helpers you tick for Little Dragons.

    Shared sign-up link

    Send this one link to a group (a chat, an email) and people find their own name to sign up. It shows the black belts you've invited (on the Invite tab) plus the extras below. No one is signed up until they pick their name.

    These are people who aren't black belts but you still want on the shared link.
